Objection Busters
Click any to expand the counter and your script angle.
01I don't have a following so brands won't work with me▼
Counter
UGC is about the video quality not your audience size. App brands buy the video and run it as their own paid ad. Your follower count is completely irrelevant.
Script angle
You do not need a social following because you do not post the content — you give it to brands. Stop applying for influencer deals and start pitching UGC deals.
02UGC is saturated, I'm too late▼
Counter
The app niche alone has thousands of brands launching every month all needing UGC content. Most creators are fighting over skincare and fashion brands — nobody is pitching gaming apps.
Script angle
The app niche has thousands of brands and almost zero competition. While everyone fights over Rhode you could be making 800 euros from a fintech app nobody else pitched.
03I make aesthetic content but brands never hire me▼
Counter
Brands buy UGC to use as paid ads. They need real people using the product authentically — not aesthetic feeds. Show your face. Show yourself using the app.
Script angle
They hire you as a USER not as an aesthetic account. The more real and scrappy it looks the better it performs as an ad.
04I don't know what to charge — I'll just go cheap▼
Counter
Two creators get the same brief. One knows about usage rights and charges 700 euros. One does not and charges 150. Same work. Completely different income.
Script angle
A brand sends the same brief to two creators. One knows to stack usage rights on top of the base rate. The other does not. That one mistake costs you 500 euros per deal.
05Brands don't reply to my pitches▼
Counter
You are pitching brands whose inboxes are already flooded. App brands in the App Store get almost no cold UGC pitches. The reply rate is completely different.
Script angle
Stop pitching brands like Rhode whose inbox is already exploding. Go to the App Store. Find apps with ads already running. Those brands have budget and nobody is pitching them.
06I need expensive gear to get started▼
Counter
App UGC is filmed on a phone by design. It needs to look native and authentic — not like a studio production. Your phone is the right tool.
Script angle
All you need is your phone. If it looks too produced it does not perform as an ad. Authentic and real always wins in this niche.
07I'm too old — brands want young creators▼
Counter
App brands want real people who genuinely use their product. A 38-year-old authentically recommending a budgeting app to other 38-year-olds is exactly the ad that converts.
Script angle
Brands do not want influencers. They want real people. And a real 38-year-old recommending a fintech app to other people like her converts better than any 22-year-old lifestyle creator.
08Physical product UGC pays nothing▼
Counter
Correct. Which is exactly why you should stop doing it. App brands have software margins, massive ad budgets, and no shipping costs. The economics are completely different.
Script angle
Why do you think Kylie Jenner promotes random gaming apps? Because they pay more than almost anyone else. Stop competing for skincare deals and go where the money actually is.
